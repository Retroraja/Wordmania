# Wordnoid privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Wordnoid is a word puzzle game by RetroRaja Games. This page explains what the game collects and why.

## What stays on your phone
Your levels, Letter Bricks, coins, boosters, Wordbook and settings are saved on your phone. Android may back them up to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup) so you keep your progress on a new phone. We do not see this data.

## Birth year
The first time you play, the game asks for your birth year. It never leaves your phone. It is only used to decide whether to show child-safe ads.

## Ads
The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded videos only play when you choose to watch one.
- Players 13 or older may see personalised ads if they agree in the consent form. You can change this any time in Settings → Ad choices.
- Players under 13 only see child-safe, non-personalised ads.
Google may collect device information (such as an advertising ID) to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Reminders
The game can send friendly reminders (for example, "Your free spin is ready"). They are made on your phone. You can switch them off in Settings.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

## Children
Wordnoid is made for players aged 13 and over. If a younger player uses the game, it shows only child-safe, non-personalised ads, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Contact
Questions? Contact RetroRaja Games through the Google Play store listing.
