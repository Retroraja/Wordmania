**Full description:**
Swap letter tiles, spell words and help Tiko bring colour back to the five lands!

The Word Eater and his villains scrambled every sign in town. Spell everyday words to win Letter Bricks, then use them to rebuild bakeries, toy shops, fountains and more.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swap two tiles to make words across or down
• Every swap costs a move, so think first!
• Finish the level goals before you run out of moves

FEATURES
• 50 hand-made levels across 5 lands: Candy, Sea, Jungle, Clouds and Galaxy
• 20 fun boosters: Hammer, Joker, Magic Swap and many more
• Boss levels: beat the villains with your words
• Rebuild your own 3D town, one shop at a time
• Daily gifts, a lucky spin and daily challenges
• Theme words, bonus words, crates and treasures
• Your own Wordbook of every word you find
• Works offline, with no login needed

Easy to learn and hard to put down. Train your brain one word at a time!
